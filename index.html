        How the maps are made. Every old territory is drawn by joining today's municipalities. This is close, but not exact: old borders did not always follow today's municipality lines. Before 1798, "How the cantons joined" uses each canton's researched history (see "Every canton"), mostly at the level of today's districts. Land outside today's Switzerland comes from OpenStreetMap.
